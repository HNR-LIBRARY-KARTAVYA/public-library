Reservations (holds)

A member reserves a book that is issued to someone else (or on the shelf). When it is returned, the Issue/Return screen shows RESERVED - keep aside and the member gets an e-mail. Ready books are kept for 3 working days.

Book Suggestions from members

Members suggest books from My Library on the website (or you add one for a reader at the counter). Each step e-mails the reader: Accept (we will try to get it), Arrived (it is here - reserve it), Reject (with your reason).
