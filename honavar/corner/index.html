Readers' Corner: Rules · ನಿಯಮಗಳು

Who can send

  1. Members of District Central Library Karwar, Branch: Honnavar with an active library card (Member ID). For the Children's Corner, a parent or guardian sends the child's work with the child's card or their own card.
  2. Up to 3 works a day for each card. Pictures up to 5 MB; written works up to 8000 letters; videos up to one minute.

What you can send

  1. Poems, stories, essays and book reviews in Kannada, English, Hindi, Konkani or any language.
  2. Photographs of nature, places, buildings, objects, animals or events, and drawings, paintings and art.
  3. Quotations that inspire you, always with the real author's name.
  4. Children's Corner: drawings, stories and poems by children up to 14 years. Only the child's first name and age are shown.

What is not allowed

  1. No people in photographs and art: no faces or persons who can be recognised, no selfies. The only exception is the Member's Pick, where you yourself may appear.
  2. Own work only: nothing copied from books, websites, social media or another person, and no work made by an AI tool.
  3. Nothing that hurts any religion, caste, community, gender, region or person; no political or party material; nothing vulgar, violent or abusive; no advertisements.
  4. No phone numbers, addresses or other personal details of anyone.
  5. Sending a work in another person's name is not allowed.

Member's Pick, VIP's Pick and Visitor's Pick

  1. Member's Pick: a photo of yourself holding the book with one line about it, or a video of up to one minute about the best book you read and why. Only you may be seen or heard. No film songs or background music. Please do not tell the ending of the book.
  2. VIP's Pick and Visitor's Pick are added only by the library, with the written permission of the guest or visitor.
  3. All picks move across the Book Suggestions Wall on this page and on the library TV screen.

How works are checked and shown

  1. The library checks every work. It may approve it, choose it as Star of the Week, or not show it. The library does not change your words. The decision of the Librarian is final.
  2. By sending a work, you allow the library to show it, free of charge, with the name, on this website, on the library TV screen, on the notice board and in library exhibitions.
  3. Your copyright stays with you. The library never sells your work. You may publish it anywhere else.
  4. You can ask the library to remove your work at any time, in person or by e-mail to dclkarwarbranchhonnavar@gmail.com. It will be removed within 7 working days.
  5. Your Member ID, phone, e-mail and address are never shown. If you do not want your name on the screen, tell the library.
  6. The library may change these rules. The latest rules are always on this page.
