Since 1988

Thirty-eight years, and still growing

Branch Honavar Library opened its doors in 1988. Every book on our shelves today, every reader who has found a quiet seat here, and every service this library offers exists because of the people who gave their working lives to build it — one issue slip, one catalogued book, one open morning at a time.

To every staff member who has served this library since 1988 — thank you. This library is your gift to Honnavar, and it will keep being read because of you.

How to Apply

Getting a library card is simple — fill the form online, then finish two quick steps in person:

  1. Fill the Online Membership Form — upload your photo, signature, and one address/ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Ration Card, Driving Licence, etc.)
  2. You'll get an auto-filled printable application by email/link — print it and get it signed by a Gazetted Officer
  3. Bring the signed form to the library with your original documents and the one-time membership fee (see left)
  4. Once approved, collect your Member ID card the same day
Apply Online →

Can't fill it online? Ask any staff member for a printed blank form — it works exactly the same way.
